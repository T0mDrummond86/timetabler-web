The **Change log** tab records every edit to the timetable — a class moved, a
lecturer swapped, a room changed, a booking added or removed.

![The Change log, with its toolbar and a net change showing what moved](/help/changelog.png)

Each row shows the action, the class, the group, the lecturer, the card ID and
any notes.

**Resolved** view collapses intermediate edits into a single net change per
booking, so a class you moved four times reads as one move from where it
started to where it ended. That is almost always what you want to send on.

The log is what drives the red markup in the
[admin export](#changelog-admin-export-red).
