The phone viewer is TAFEtabler on a small screen, at the `/m` address. The
Dashboard's **Timetables on your phone** panel has the QR code and the link.

It is for **you**, not for lecturers — it needs a sign-in like everything else,
and it shows **any** lecturer's week across the workspace, not just one
person's. That is the point: it answers "who is free at eleven" while you are
standing in a corridor rather than sitting at a desk.

On the phone you can:

- pick **whose week to show** from the full list of lecturers, and search it;
- tap a class for its full detail;
- see who is free right now;
- [arrange cover](#cover-from-phone) and push it to the shared cover log.

**Install this app** adds it to the home screen, so it opens full-screen and
keeps working in a dead spot.

It is read-only for the timetable itself: classes cannot be moved from the
phone. Cover is the one thing it can write.
