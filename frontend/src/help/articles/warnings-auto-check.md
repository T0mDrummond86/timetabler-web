**Display ▾** has two related controls:

- **Auto clash detect** — when on, clashes are re-checked after every move. When
  off, they are not.
- **Check clashes** — runs one full check on the current week, on demand.

On a very large session, turning auto-detect off makes dragging feel quicker;
run **Check clashes** when you want the picture brought up to date.

**Show alerts** in the same menu controls whether the coloured borders appear
on placecards at all.
