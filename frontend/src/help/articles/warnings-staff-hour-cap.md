**Soft warning.** The lecturer is over their maximum hours per week, counted per
term.

Move some teaching to another lecturer, or check whether their cap and FTE are
recorded correctly on the **Staff** tab. The
[variance filter](#staff-hours-variance) set to *Underloaded* is a quick way to
find someone who could take the class instead.
