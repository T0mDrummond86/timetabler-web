**Hard clash.** The class is scheduled outside the lecturer's recorded
availability.

Either move the class into a time they are available, or — if the availability
record is wrong — correct it on their
[availability grid](#staff-availability).

Availability often comes in from the
[lecturer preferences import](#import-lecturer-preferences), so a wave of these
warnings just after an import usually means the spreadsheet's windows need
checking rather than the timetable.
