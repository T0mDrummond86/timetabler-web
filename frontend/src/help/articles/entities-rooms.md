The **Rooms** tab holds every room the session can schedule into.

Each room has a **code**, a **capacity** (how many students it seats) and a
**type**. Capacity and type feed two clash checks:
[Room too small](#warnings-room-too-small) and
[Wrong room type](#warnings-room-type).

A room code is required and must be unique.

Online delivery is handled with rooms too — an online "room" carries a cohort
without taking a physical space, which is why online classes are excluded from
the in-class contact figure in [lecturer hours](#staff-hours-variance).
