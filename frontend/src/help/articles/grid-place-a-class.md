Classes waiting to be scheduled sit in the **holding area** below the grid.

1. Find the class in the holding area.
2. Drag it onto the grid, into the group column and time you want.
3. Drop it. The placecard appears and the class leaves the holding area.

You can also click an empty slot in the grid to create a booking there and pick
the class from the dialog.

Once placed, assign a lecturer and a room by clicking the placecard — see
[Editing a placecard](#grid-edit-placecard).

If the drop is refused, check you are not in
[read-only access](#access-read-only) — the message *Read-only access — you
cannot schedule classes* appears on the holding area.
