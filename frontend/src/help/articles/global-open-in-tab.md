title: Getting to the global workspace without losing your place
category: Global workspace
keywords: open global, second tab, duplicate tab, navigate to global, switch workspace, lose my place, two tabs
---

The global workspace is a separate screen from your session, so moving to it
takes you away from whatever you were doing.

The easy answer is a second browser tab: **duplicate the current tab**
(right-click the tab → *Duplicate*, or Cmd/Ctrl-click the link), then navigate
the copy to the workspace. You end up with the session in one tab and the
workspace in the other, and can flick between them.

From the Dashboard, the workspace is under **Global sessions**.
