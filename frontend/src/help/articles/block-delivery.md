Most qualifications repeat weekly across the semester. A **block** qualification
runs as a 1–3 week intensive instead.

Set **Delivery mode** to *block* on the qualification, then give it a **block
week count** and a **block start semester week**.

Block cohorts are separate from the regular group courses. **Create block** on
the qualification editor makes one, and the **Block delivery** panel lists the
block groups and which block week each is in.

Errors like *Not a block qualification* or *block_week_index is required for
block delivery* mean an action meant for a block cohort has landed on a regular
one, or the week has not been chosen.
