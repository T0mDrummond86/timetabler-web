When you turn on two-factor you are given a set of one-time **recovery codes**.
They are displayed **once**. Save them somewhere safe and away from your phone —
a password manager is ideal.

Each code works once, in place of an authenticator code.

**If you have lost your phone and your codes**, you cannot get back in on your
own. An administrator has to clear your two-factor so you can set it up on a new
phone — see [Managing users](#admin-users).
