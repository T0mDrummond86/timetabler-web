Competency links a lecturer to the classes they are able to deliver. It works
in both directions:

- From the **Staff** tab, tick the classes a lecturer *can do*.
- From the **Classes** tab, tick the lecturers allowed on a class.

When you assign a lecturer to a placecard, competent lecturers are offered
first. Assigning someone who is not on the class's list raises
[Lecturer not on allowed list](#warnings-lecturer-not-allowed) — a **soft**
warning, so it will not block you, but it will show up in the warnings report.
