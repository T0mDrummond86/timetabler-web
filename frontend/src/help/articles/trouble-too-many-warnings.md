category: Troubleshooting

A freshly imported session often lights up. Work through it like this:

1. Go to the **Warnings** tab and filter to **Hard (clashes / blocks) only**.
   Those are the real problems; soft warnings can wait.
2. Turn off checks that do not apply at your campus in
   [Clash settings](#warnings-clash-settings). Many soft scheduling rules —
   idle gaps, daily hour floors — are site preferences, not rules.
3. If the noise is mostly *Staff unavailable*, the
   [preferences import](#import-lecturer-preferences) probably needs checking
   before the timetable does.
4. Turn off **Show alerts** in **Display ▾** while you concentrate on placement,
   and turn it back on to review.
