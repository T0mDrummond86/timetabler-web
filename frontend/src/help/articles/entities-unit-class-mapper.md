The **Unit-class mapper** turns a pasted list of unit codes into the class names
this session uses.

Paste your unit codes into the box. The panel matches them against the
session's qualifications and shows the classes those units are clustered into,
in the form `Class (unit1, unit2)`.

**Copy to clipboard** takes the output away for a spreadsheet or an email.

It is a read-only lookup — nothing in the session changes.
