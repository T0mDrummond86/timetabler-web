The **Staff** tab holds every lecturer in the session.

Select one to edit their details, including **FTE**, **cost centre** and the
non-teaching allowances that feed [variance](#staff-hours-variance).

**New staff member** adds one; a name is required.

From here you also reach their [availability](#staff-availability) and
[competencies](#staff-competency).
