The **Clash settings** tab lists every check, grouped into:

- **Double-booking & clashes**
- **Staff rules**
- **Room rules**
- **Qualification**
- **Scheduling rules**

Tick a check to have it enforced, untick it to stop it being reported. This is
the right answer when a rule does not apply at your campus and is drowning out
the ones that do.

Switching a check off does not fix anything — it stops it being reported. The
underlying booking is unchanged.

There is also **Re-detect combined classes** here, for when classes that should
be recognised as taught together have fallen out of step.
