title: Move, resize or remove a placecard
category: The timetable grid
keywords: move class, drag placecard, change time, resize, longer, shorter, delete booking, remove, unschedule
---

**Move** — drag the placecard to a new time or a new group column.

**Remove** — right-click the placecard and choose **Delete (return to
holding)**. The class goes back to the holding area, ready to place again.
**Delete placecard** removes it outright.

**Duration** — a class's length comes from the class itself, not the
placecard. Change it on the **Classes** tab and every placecard for that class
follows.

If a move creates a clash the placecard picks up a coloured border — see
[Why a placecard has a red or orange border](#warnings-card-borders).
