---
id: warnings-staff-double
title: "Staff double-booking"
category: Warnings and clashes
keywords: lecturer double booked, teacher in two places, staff clash, same lecturer twice, overlapping classes
---

**Hard clash.** The same lecturer is assigned to two classes that overlap in
time.

Move one class, or give one of them a different lecturer.

Online cohorts are the exception: a permitted overlap between online cohorts
does not raise this, because one lecturer can hold two online groups at once.
