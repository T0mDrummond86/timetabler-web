These checks watch the shape of the week rather than direct clashes.

**Hard:**

- **Friday finish after 18:00** — a class finishing after 6pm on Friday.
- **6-hour teaching without break** — a lecturer teaching more than 6 hours in a
  row with no 30-minute break.

**Soft:**

- **Monday start before 09:30**.
- **Staff idle gap over 2 hours** — a long gap between a lecturer's classes in a
  day.
- **Staff under 5 hours** / **over 9 hours** on a teaching day.
- **Student cohort under 5 hours** / **over 8 hours** in a day.
- **Student break over 2 hours** — a long gap for a cohort in a day.

All of them can be switched off individually in
[Clash settings](#warnings-clash-settings) if they do not match how your campus
runs.
