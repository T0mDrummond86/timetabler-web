TAFEtabler is a **management tool**. It is used by the people who build and
maintain timetables — not by the lecturers being timetabled.

**Lecturers do not get accounts and do not sign in.** Every account is created
by an administrator (see [Managing users](#admin-users)), and every account
holder is someone doing timetabling work. There is no self-registration.

Lecturers appear throughout the app as **data** — their availability, their
competencies, their hours, their cover balance — but they are the subject of the
timetable, not users of it.

What lecturers actually receive is output you send them:

- printed or PDF timetables — see [Printing](#export-print);
- the change log emailed to them when something moves, naming each affected
  lecturer — see [Sending the change log](#changelog-copy-email);
- cover requests emailed from the [Lecturer cover](#cover-arrange) tab.

The [phone viewer](#phone-app) is also a management tool: it needs an account
and shows any lecturer's week, which is what makes it useful when you are away
from a desk.

The nearest thing to a restricted account is
[read-only access](#access-read-only), which is for colleagues who need to look
at a timetable without changing it — still management, just not editing.
