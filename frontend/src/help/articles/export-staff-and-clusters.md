Two more spreadsheets live in **Export ▾**:

**Staff tab** — the lecturer hours spreadsheet, matching the figures in
[hours and variance](#staff-hours-variance).

**Qualification clusters…** — opens a picker. Choose the qualifications you
want and press **Download workbook**; each comes out as its own tab with its
clusters and units. *Select at least one qualification* means nothing is ticked.
