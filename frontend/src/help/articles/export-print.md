**Export ▾ → Print timetables…** builds an A4 landscape PDF.

Choose what to include:

- **Courses (class group timetables)**
- **Staff timetables**
- **Room timetables**
- **Courses and staff**
- **Changed courses (from change log)** — only what has moved

and which terms: **All terms** or **Term 1 only**.

**Class colours** carries the grid's colours onto the page. Then **Download
PDF**.

*No timetables selected* means nothing is ticked yet.
