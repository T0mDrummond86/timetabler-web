Passphrases must be **at least 14 characters**. Four random words is the easiest
way to get there — *wattle plover sundial kelpie* is both far stronger and far
easier to type than *P@ssw0rd1*.

There are deliberately **no upper/lower/digit/symbol rules**, and nothing ever
expires. Spaces are fine.

A passphrase is refused if it is:

- shorter than 14 characters, or longer than 128;
- a commonly used one, or a common word with a little added;
- one or two characters repeated, like `abababababababab`;
- a long keyboard or alphabet run, like `qwertyuiopasdfgh`;
- built from your own name or username.

To change it, use **Change password**. *New password must be different from the
current password* means you have re-entered the old one.
