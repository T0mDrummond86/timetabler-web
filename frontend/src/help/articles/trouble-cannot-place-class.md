id: trouble-cannot-place-class
title: A class will not drop onto the grid
category: Troubleshooting
keywords: cannot drop, will not place, drag not working, refuses, cannot schedule, locked, read only, nothing happens
---

Check in this order:

1. **Read-only access.** The holding area says *Read-only access — you cannot
   schedule classes*. See [Read-only access](#access-read-only).
2. **The card is locked.** A locked placecard will not move — see
   [Locking](#grid-locking).
3. **You are in split view.** Split view has no holding area; drag from the
   normal timetable view first. See [Split view](#grid-split-view).
4. **The slot is blocked.** Time a shared lecturer is teaching in another
   session shows as unavailable — see
   [Shared lecturers](#global-shared-lecturer).

A clash does **not** prevent a drop. It places the card and flags it, so you can
sort the conflict out afterwards.
