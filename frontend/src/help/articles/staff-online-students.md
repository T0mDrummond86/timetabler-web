Online delivery is charged against a lecturer by headcount rather than by room.

You can set a per-lecturer student count against a qualification, and a
per-booking count on an individual class. Where nothing is set, a default of 20
is assumed.

These numbers drive the **Online / collaborate load hours** column in the
[hours table](#staff-hours-variance), and there is a *Per-class online session
detail* breakdown if you need to see where a figure came from.
