title: I cannot sign in
category: Troubleshooting
keywords: cannot sign in, login failed, wrong password, locked out, code not accepted, disabled, forgot password, access denied
---

- *Invalid username or password* — check both. Usernames are lowercase.
- *That code was not accepted* — your phone's clock has probably drifted; turn
  on automatic time. Five wrong codes locks the account for 15 minutes.
- *Account is disabled* — an administrator has to re-enable it.
- **Forgotten passphrase** — an administrator resets it; see
  [Managing users](#admin-users).
- **Lost phone** — use a [recovery code](#account-recovery-codes), or ask an
  administrator to clear your two-factor.
- *Registration is disabled; contact an administrator* — accounts are created by
  administrators, not self-signup.
