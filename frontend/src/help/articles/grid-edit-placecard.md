![A placecard showing the time, class name, lecturer and room](/help/placecard.png)

Click a placecard to open its editor. From there you can set:

- **Lecturer** — who teaches it. Lecturers who are competent in the class are
  offered first; see [Allowed lecturers](#staff-competency).
- **Room** — where it runs. *Available rooms* lists what is free at that time.
- **Notes** — free text shown on the card.
- **Card ID** — your own reference for this specific booking. The same class
  can carry a different ID in each group.
- **Terms** — whether the class runs in term 1, term 2, or both. A booking must
  be active in at least one term.

Right-clicking a placecard gives the same options plus the quick actions
described in [Locking](#grid-locking).

## Copy session details

**Copy session details** on the right-click menu puts the class, its units, the
group, lecturer, room, day and time on the clipboard as a small table. Paste it
straight into an email — it arrives as a table, not as a run of text.

It works on any placecard in any view, and says *Not assigned* where a lecturer
or room is still missing rather than leaving a blank someone has to interpret.
The second sitting of a [double class](#entities-classes) is labelled as such,
so two copies of the same class are told apart.
