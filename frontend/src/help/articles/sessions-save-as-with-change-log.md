Settings offers two ways to copy a session:

- **Save as…** — a plain duplicate. The copy starts with a clean change log.
- **Save as with change log…** — the copy also inherits the change history, so
  the red markup in an [admin export](#changelog-admin-export-red) still shows
  what moved.

Use the second when the copy is the version you will hand on to
administrators, and the first when you just want a scratch pad.
