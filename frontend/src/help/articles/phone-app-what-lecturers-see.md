Lecturers get a read-only view of their own week on their phone, at the `/m`
address. The Dashboard's **Timetables on your phone** panel has the QR code and
link to send them.

On the phone they can:

- see their week, and tap a class for full detail;
- pick whose week to show, where they have permission;
- see who is free now;
- [arrange cover](#cover-from-phone) and send it to the cover log.

**Install this app** adds it to the home screen so it opens like a native app
and works offline.

It is read-only for the timetable itself: lecturers cannot move classes.
