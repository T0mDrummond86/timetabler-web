title: "Qualification time window"
category: Warnings and clashes
keywords: qualification window, outside window, day night, too early, too late, wrong time, schedule period
---

**Hard clash.** The class is scheduled outside the day/time window its
qualification allows.

Qualifications are either **Day** (08:30–19:00) or **Night** (17:30–21:30). A
class must fit **entirely** inside the window — a class that starts at 18:30 and
runs two hours will not fit a day qualification.

Either move the class inside the window, or change the qualification's
**Schedule period** on the [Qualifications](#entities-qualifications) tab.

Note that a class belonging to two qualifications must satisfy both windows.
This is a common surprise after a [merge](#quals-merge), and the merge dialog
warns about it.
