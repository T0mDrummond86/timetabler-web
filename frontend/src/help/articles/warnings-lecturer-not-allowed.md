**Soft warning.** The assigned lecturer is not on the class's allowed-lecturers
list.

It will not stop you scheduling — sometimes the right person genuinely is not on
the list yet. Fix it either by choosing a different lecturer, or by adding this
one to the class's list; see [Allowed lecturers](#staff-competency).
