Two-factor sign-in is **required for every account**. The first time you sign
in, you are taken straight to **Set up two-factor sign-in** and cannot go
further until it is done.

1. Open an authenticator app on your phone — Google Authenticator, Microsoft
   Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, any of them.
2. Scan the QR code. If you cannot scan, type the key shown beneath it in by
   hand.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows and press **Turn on two-factor**.
4. **Save your recovery codes.** They are shown once and never again — see
   [Recovery codes](#account-recovery-codes).

After that, signing in asks for your passphrase and then a code.

*That code was not accepted* almost always means your phone's clock has drifted.
Turn on automatic time on the phone and try again.
