The **Staff** tab's hours table shows each lecturer's workload.

![The lecturer hours table, with a green over-hours and a red under-hours variance badge](/help/staff-hours.png)

- **In-class contact** — weekly scheduled hours in non-online rooms, adjusted
  for the session.
- **Online / collaborate load** — hours from online cohorts.
- **Non-teaching allowances** — supervision, PD/training, development, and any
  non-teaching day.
- **Total workload** — all of the above.
- **Variance** — *total workload minus lecturing hours*, where lecturing hours
  are **FTE × 21**.

So a full-time lecturer is expected to carry 21 hours, and variance is how far
above or below that they sit.

The **Variance filter** narrows the table to **Overloaded**, **Underloaded** or
**Balanced** lecturers — the fastest way to find who has room to take more work,
or who needs some taken off them.

## Hours owed, and owed after cover

Two further columns turn a weekly shortfall into what the lecturer owes over the
whole semester.

- **Hours owed** — the weekly shortfall multiplied by the semester (20 weeks).
  A lecturer 2 hours under each week owes 40 hours.
- **Owed after cover** — the same figure once the cover jobs on the shared
  [cover log](#global-cover-log) are credited. A settled debt shows a green
  **0**; it never goes negative, because covering more than you owe makes you
  square rather than in credit.

Both are **blank for anyone on or over their hours** — only a shortfall is
tracked.

They are also blank when a lecturer teaches in more than one linked session and
those sessions disagree about their variance, because there is then no single
figure to report. The fix is to make the non-teaching allowances match across
the sessions, not to read the number off one of them.

The figures are worked out from the lecturer's **combined** load across every
linked session, so they match what the [Lecturer cover](#cover-arrange) tab
shows for the same person.
