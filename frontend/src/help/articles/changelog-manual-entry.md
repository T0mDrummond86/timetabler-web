Not every change happens in the app. The manual change-log dialog lets you
record one that happened elsewhere, so the export tells the whole story.

Choose the booking, tick which fields to log, and add your note. *Select at
least one field to log* means nothing is ticked yet.

Manual entries appear in the log and mark up in the
[admin export](#changelog-admin-export-red) like any other change.
