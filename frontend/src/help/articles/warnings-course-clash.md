category: Warnings and clashes
keywords: course clash, group clash, students in two places, cohort overlap, two classes same group, double booked students
---

**Hard clash.** Two classes for the **same group** overlap in time — meaning
that cohort of students would have to be in two places at once.

Move one of the two. Because both are in the same column on the grid, an
overlap here is usually easy to spot visually.
