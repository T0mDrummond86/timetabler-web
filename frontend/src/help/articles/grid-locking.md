title: Lock a class so it cannot move
category: The timetable grid
keywords: lock, locked, pin, freeze, cannot move, lock time, lock lecturer, padlock, protect
---

Right-click a placecard for two locks:

- **Lock time** — the class keeps its day and time.
- **Lock lecturer** — the class keeps its assigned lecturer.

Locked cards show a padlock and refuse to be dragged. Use the same menu items
(now reading **Unlock time** / **Unlock lecturer**) to release them.

Locks also protect a card when the timetable is rebuilt or cleared, which makes
them the right tool for anything already published to students.

A whole group can be locked at once: on the **Groups** editor, tick
*timetable locked* for that cohort.
