**CSP** stands for **course study plan**. The Word (`.docx`) variant is imported
with **Import ▾ → Qualifications CSP**.

The importer reads the plan's tables and creates or updates the qualifications
and the classes underneath them, one qualification per stage.

Common problems:

- *Please upload a Word .docx file* — the file is not a `.docx`. A `.doc` or a
  PDF will not work.
- *No CSP qualification tables found in …* — the document does not contain the
  tables the importer looks for. Check you have the study plan itself and not a
  cover sheet or a summary.

For the spreadsheet form of a CSP, see
[Importing an EP-NB CSP](#import-ep-nb-csp).
