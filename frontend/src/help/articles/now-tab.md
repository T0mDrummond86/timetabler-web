The **Now** tab answers the question you get asked in corridors: who is teaching
at this moment, and who is free.

It reads the current day and time against the timetable, so it needs no setup.
Use it to find someone who can step in at short notice — then arrange it
properly on the [Lecturer cover](#cover-arrange) tab.
