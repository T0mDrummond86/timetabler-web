Large sessions do most of their work when clashes are re-checked.

- Turn **Auto clash detect** off in **Display ▾** while you do a run of moves,
  then press **Check clashes** once at the end.
- Turn **Show alerts** off to stop the grid re-drawing borders.
- Narrow the sidebar selection to the groups you are actually working on.
- [Zoom out](#grid-zoom) less — a very wide grid draws more.

See [Automatic clash checking](#warnings-auto-check).
