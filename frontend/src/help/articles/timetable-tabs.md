Once a session is open, the tab strip runs across the top:

**Timetable** — the week grid where you place classes.
**Now** — who is teaching right now, and who is free.
**Change log** — every edit, and what to send to administrators.
**Lecturer cover** — arranging cover for an absent lecturer.
**Unit-class mapper** — turning a pasted unit-code list into class names.

Then a second group of setup tabs:

**Warnings** · **Staff** · **Classes** · **Qualifications** · **Rooms** ·
**Clash settings** · **Class custodians**

If a tab is missing, you may have read-only access to the session — see
[Read-only access](#access-read-only).
