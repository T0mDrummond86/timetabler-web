The **Cover log** tab of the workspace is the record of every cover job across
all linked sessions.

Push a request to it from the [Lecturer cover](#cover-arrange) tab of a session,
or from the [phone app](#cover-from-phone).

Each entry carries the shortfall figures: *What this lecturer still owed before
this job*, *What they still owed once this job was done*, and — where it applies
— *This job cleared what they owed*. Because the log is shared, a lecturer's
balance follows them between sessions.

**Cover hours done** totals what has been paid back.
