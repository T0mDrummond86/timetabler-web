The copy button on the Lecturer cover tab — *Copy the pending cover requests as
a table, ready to paste into an email* — puts the **pending cover requests** on
the clipboard as a table.

The columns include the class, group, day and time, the away lecturer, the
cover lecturer, and their **Hours owed** and **Owed after cover**.

Paste it straight into an email; it arrives as a table, not as raw text.

This copies the cover list, not the lecturer's own timetable.
