A lecturer teaching in more than one session looks free in each one taken alone.
The workspace fixes that.

On the workspace's **Staff** tab, a shared lecturer's aggregated week shows every
commitment across the linked sessions. Time they are teaching elsewhere shows as
busy, so you can see the slot you were about to use is not actually free.

Back in a session, those elsewhere-commitments appear on the grid as blocked
time, which is what stops you scheduling over them.
