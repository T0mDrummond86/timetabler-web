Split view puts two timetables next to each other — two groups, a group and a
lecturer, or a lecturer and a room — which is the quickest way to find a time
that suits both.

Open it from the timetable toolbar, then choose what each side shows.

**There is no holding area in split view.** To bring an unscheduled class in,
switch back to the normal timetable view, drag it out of the holding area
there, and then return to split view to fine-tune the placement.
