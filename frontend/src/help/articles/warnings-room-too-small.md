title: "Room too small"
category: Warnings and clashes
keywords: room too small, capacity, not enough seats, students do not fit, room capacity, small room, overcrowded
---

**Hard clash.** The room's capacity is below the class's required capacity.

Move the class to a bigger room, or correct the numbers: room capacity is on the
**Rooms** tab, required capacity on the **Classes** tab.

The check is named *Room too small* in Clash settings — if you are looking for
"room capacity", that is the one.
