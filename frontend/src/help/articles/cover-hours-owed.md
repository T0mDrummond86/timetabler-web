Cover is tracked as a running balance per lecturer.

- **Hours owed** — what this lecturer still owes before the job.
- **Owed after cover** — what they would still owe once this cover is done.

A lecturer who is under their hours can pay some back by covering, so picking
the person who owes most is usually the fair choice. When a job clears the
balance entirely, the row says *This cover clears what they owe*.

The same two figures appear on the shared [cover log](#global-cover-log), so a
lecturer's balance follows them across every session in the workspace.

## Adjusting what a cover is worth

The **Hours** box on each pending request starts at the length of the class
being covered, which is right most of the time. Type over it when the
arrangement and the timetable disagree — someone taking only the back half of a
three-hour class, a session that finished early, or two people splitting one
cover between them.

The figure you set is the figure that gets logged, and **Owed after cover**
recalculates as you go, so the payback shown is the payback that actually
lands.

Clear the box to go back to the class's own length. Editing anything else on
the row — the cover lecturer, the date — leaves an adjusted figure alone, and
[repeating the request](#cover-repeat-week) into the next week carries it
across.
