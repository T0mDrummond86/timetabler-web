title: Repeat a cover plan into the next week
category: Lecturer cover
keywords: repeat, next week, duplicate cover, another week, extend cover, longer absence, copy forward
---

When an absence runs longer than a week, you do not have to build the plan
twice.

The repeat button — *Copy the last week of this plan forward by one week* —
duplicates the listed cover plan a further week ahead. Press it again for each
additional week.

Each repeat copies the **last** week of the plan, so pressing it three times
gives you three consecutive covered weeks.
