The **Warnings** tab lists every violation in the session in one place, rather
than making you hunt for coloured borders.

![The Warnings tab, listing violations with the hard and soft filter](/help/warnings-report.png)

Filter it with the dropdown:

- **All warnings**
- **Hard (clashes / blocks) only**
- **Soft warnings only**

Each row names the rule, the booking and the people or rooms involved, so you
can work down the list. The count also appears as a chip near the toolbar,
which is the quickest way to tell whether the week is clean.
