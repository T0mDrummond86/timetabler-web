A **session** is one complete timetable — one campus, one semester's worth of
planning. Everything lives inside a session: its lecturers, rooms, classes,
groups, qualifications, bookings and change log.

Sessions are independent. Editing one never affects another, so you can keep
last semester's timetable alongside the one you are building, or run a
"what-if" copy beside the real thing.

Sessions appear on the **Dashboard** under *Timetable sessions*, with the ones
you have opened lately under *Recently opened*.

Several sessions can be joined into a **global workspace** when they share
lecturers — see [Global workspace](#global-what-is-it).
