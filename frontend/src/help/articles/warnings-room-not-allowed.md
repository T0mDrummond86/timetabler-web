id: warnings-room-not-allowed
title: "Room not on allowed list"
category: Warnings and clashes
keywords: room not allowed, wrong room, restricted room, allowed rooms, room list, cannot use this room
---

**Hard clash.** The room is not on the class's allowed-rooms list.

Either move the class into one of the allowed rooms, or widen the list on the
**Classes** tab — see [Restricting rooms](#entities-allowed-rooms). Clearing the
list entirely removes the restriction.
