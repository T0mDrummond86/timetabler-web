A **global workspace** joins several timetable sessions that share lecturers.

It exists because one lecturer often teaches across more than one session, and
none of those sessions can see the others. The workspace is the layer that can:

- show a shared lecturer's **whole** commitment, not just their part in one
  session;
- keep one [cover log](#global-cover-log) with a running hours-owed balance;
- keep one [custodian list](#global-custodians) across sessions;
- hold the shared [term calendar](#import-calendar).

Open it from the **Global sessions** list on the Dashboard.
